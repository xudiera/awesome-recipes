# Pizza Dough

## Ingredients

- 325 ml warm water
- 30 ml olive oil
- 425 g all-purpose flour
- 10 g salt
- 7 g active dry yeast

## Instructions

1. Add water and active dry yeast to the bowl, mix until dissolved. Set aside.
2. Gradually add the remaining ingredients (olive oil, flour, and salt) to the bowl, mixing well until well combined.
3. Cover the dough with plastic wrap and let it rest for 30 minutes.
4. Do a set of stretching.
5. Repeat steps 3 & 4 two more times.
6. Once the dough has rested for the last 30 minutes, spread flour in a flat surface to keep the dough from sticking.
7. Divided the dough into four equal parts.
8. Shape the four portions into a ball and let it rest for 15 minutes.
9. Flatten the balls into a pizza shape.
10. Add the toppings.
11. Bake at 400°F (205°C) for 20 minutes or until the crust is golden brown.
