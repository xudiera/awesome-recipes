# Sourdough Bread

## Ingredients
- 500g bread flour
- 325g water
- 100g sourdough starter
- 10g salt

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, mix water and sourdough starter.
2. Add the flour and mix by hand until fully combined.
3. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
4. Add a splash of water, then pinch the salt into the dough. Mix until fully combined.
5. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
6. Perform three coil folds. Rest for 30 minutes between each fold.
7. Cover and rest for bulk fermentation (on the counter).
8. Wait until the dough doubles in size (this can take 3-6 hours).
9. After the dough has doubled, shape your dough and place it in a floured banneton.
10. Place it in the fridge overnight.
11. Preheat your dutch oven to 500°F for 1 hour.
12. Take the bannetons out of the fridge.
13. Prepare parchment paper and place the boule or batard onto the parchment paper.
14. Sprinkle a little flour on top and score.
15. Place it in the preheated Dutch oven.
16. Bake for 20 minutes at 500°F with the lid on.
17. Bake for 15-20 minutes at 425°F with the lid off.
