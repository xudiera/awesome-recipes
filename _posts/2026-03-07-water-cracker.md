# Water Cracker

## Ingredients

- 200 ml warm water
- 6 g active dry yeast
- 300 g all-purpose flour
- 5 g salt
- 60 g butter at room temperature

## Instructions

1. Combine the warm water and yeast in a bowl. Let sit for 5 minutes, or until foamy.
2. Mix the flour, salt, and butter with the water mixture and knead until a soft ball forms.
3. Cover with plastic wrap and let the dough rest and rise for 30 minutes.
4. Cover with plastic wrap and let the dough rise for up to 1 hours.
5. Stretch the dough out on a floured surface to about 2 mm thickness.
6. Preheat the oven to 380°F.
7. Cut the dough into desired shapes using a cookie cutter or knife.
8. Place the cut dough on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
9. Bake for 15-30 minutes, or until the crackers are golden brown and crisp
10. Remove from the oven and let cool on a wire rack before serving.
