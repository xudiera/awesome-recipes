# White Bread

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (240 ml) warm water
- 2 tbsp oil or melted butter
- 2 1/2 Cups (300 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp (12 g) granulated sugar
- 1 tsp (4 g) salt
- 1 tsp (4 g) active dry yeast

## Instructions

1. Add the ingredients to the bread machine in the order listed (the yeast should not be mixed with the salt).
