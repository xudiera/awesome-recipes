# Orange Loaf Cake

## Ingredients

- 1 Orange
- 3/4 Cup of Sugar
- 2 Eggs
- 2 Cup of All Purpose Flour
- 1 tbsp of Baking Powder
- Pinch of Salt
- 7 tbsp of oil
- 1 tsp of Vanilla
- 4 tbsp of buttermilk, milk, or plain yogurt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 380°F.
2. Submerge the orange in boiling water for at least 10 minutes.
3. Cut the orange into small pieces (remove the core and seeds of the orange).
4. In a large bowl, beat together the orange, eggs, oil, milk, and sugar until blended.
5. Add the flour, baking powder, salt, and vanilla extract and mix until well combined (don't over mix).
6. Prepare the baking pan by lightly greasing it with butter or cover with parchment paper.
7. Add the mixture to the prepared pan.
8. Bake for about 45 minutes, or until the cake is golden brown.
9. Optionally mix 1/4 of cup of orange juice with 2 tsp of sugar and pour into the cake.
10. Let the cake cool in the pan for a few minutes before serving.
